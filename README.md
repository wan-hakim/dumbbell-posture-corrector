# dumbbell-posture-corrector

[A system that provides automated real-time workout posture monitoring and immediate corrective feedback for beginners, home fitness enthusiasts, and students performing dumbbell exercises without professional supervision.]

## Problem statement
[Beginners and home fitness enthusiasts often perform resistance training, specifically dumbbell exercises, with improper posture and movement errors due to a lack of immediate expert feedback and kinesthetic awareness. Performing these exercises with incorrect form reduces muscle engagement and significantly increases the risk of serious musculoskeletal injuries, particularly to the spine and rotator cuff. Hiring personal trainers is often too expensive or impractical for home workouts, while existing computer vision solutions either rely on computationally heavy models that cause high processing latency on standard consumer devices or use simple classification that fails to track continuous physical movements.]
